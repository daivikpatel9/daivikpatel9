About the production

I was the editor and sound designer, and I had a major part in post-production: cutting the film together and building its sound.

My homelab was part of the production. I edited the film on my own server, and Nextcloud, the file cloud running on it, was the sharing hub during production. Many of the clips were stored there so the crew could pass footage back and forth, and the finished file is kept there too. Those files are private and are not hosted publicly.

I am credited as Editor and Sound Designer. The full credits are in the film itself.

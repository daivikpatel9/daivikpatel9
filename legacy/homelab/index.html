Homelab · Overview

A home server where about twenty systems act as one

I run a home server that hosts my own cameras, photos, files and media, and ties them to the devices in my house. No single app is the project. The project is the wiring between them, and Home Assistant is where the pieces meet.

The system map

Everything runs on one server under Unraid, an operating system for home servers that runs each service in its own container. Home Assistant, open-source home automation software, sits in the middle. Signals come in from devices made by different companies, and Home Assistant sends actions out to others. Most of my automations start with one brand and end with another.

Follow one event: someone comes to the door

One visitor passes through most of the system. Each step links to the tab that explains it.

  1. DetectThe doorbell camera sees a person
  2. LightThe outside light turns on and the doorbell switches to color night mode (automations)
  3. ScreensA chime plays and the tablet and TV switch to live cameras for 90 seconds
  4. AlertFrigate sends a person alert to our phones
  5. ReturnThe screens go back to the photo slideshow served from the server
